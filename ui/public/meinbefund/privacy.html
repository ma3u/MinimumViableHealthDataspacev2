MeinBefund, Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026. Controller: Matthias Buchhorn. Contact: the repository issue tracker at github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2.

MeinBefund reads your paper laboratory reports and keeps the values on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose, per question, to have a value explained, and you are shown exactly which values would leave before anything does.

The store is excluded from iCloud Backup. Health data is not written to iCloud and is not written to Apple Health.

Special category data

Laboratory values are data concerning health, a special category under Art. 9 GDPR. Processing on your device happens because you chose to scan the report. Sending values for an explanation happens only on your explicit consent, given for each individual request, under Art. 9(2)(a).

When you ask for an explanation

You pick the values, you see them listed, and you confirm. Only those values travel, together with your question. Your name, date of birth, insurance number and the scanned image are never sent, because the app does not hold the first three at all and does not transmit the fourth.

Where a request goes through the app provider's service, the provider is a processor and the model vendor a sub-processor. Neither uses your values to train models. Choosing the United States option is an international transfer, and the app tells you so at the moment you choose it.

Signing in, and why

Signing in is required only for the default service, and only so that the daily limit of 20 analyses can be counted per person. The counter stores a one-way hash of your account identifier and a number. It does not store your name or email.

If you configure your own provider, no sign-in is required and no account is created, because there is nothing to meter.

Logs

The service logs the number of values in a request and their LOINC codes, so faults can be diagnosed. It does not log the measured values, your question, or the answer.

What the app never does

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of health data, and no use of it for data mining.
  • No storage of health data in iCloud.
  • No account is needed to scan, read and keep your reports.

Accuracy, and what this app is not

Values are read from a photograph by optical character recognition and can be misread. They are marked preliminary until you check them against the paper. Reference ranges are shown exactly as your laboratory printed them and are never replaced with ranges from elsewhere.

MeinBefund does not diagnose, does not assess risk and does not recommend treatment. It explains what a measurement is. It is not a medical device and it is not a substitute for your doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making any decision about your health.

Your rights

Because the data lives on your device, you exercise most rights directly: delete a report in the app and it is gone. For anything held by the service, the daily counter, contact the address above. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under Arts. 15 to 21 GDPR, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

Retention

Reports stay until you delete them or delete the app. The daily counter holds a hash and a number per day and is not needed beyond the day it counts.

Children

MeinBefund is not directed at children and is intended for adults managing their own laboratory results.
