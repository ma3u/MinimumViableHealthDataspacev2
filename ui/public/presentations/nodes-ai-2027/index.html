THE PROBLEM

From 2029, health data is open to research. On four conditions.

Chapter IV of the EHDS Regulation (EU) 2025/327. An AI assistant over that data has to keep the same four, on every query.

1

A permit

A Health Data Access Body decides each application (Art. 67, 68).

2

An opt-out

Citizens may refuse secondary use of their data (Art. 71).

3

A secure environment

Analysis happens inside it; only results leave (Art. 73).

4

Nobody identified

Re-identifying a person is prohibited (Art. 61, 66).

[2:00] Permit, opt-out, secure environment, no re-identification. Whoever or whatever writes the query.

OPTIONAL MATCH (p)-[:OFFERS|CONSUMES]->(:DataProduct)

WHAT EHDS BUILDERS CAN LEARN FROM GRAPH PEOPLE

          
Five lessons

          

            

              1
              
One graph across standards
beats one pipeline per standard. FHIR, OMOP and DCAT meet in edges, not in ETL.

            

            

              2
              
Ground on codes, not text
SNOMED CT and LOINC nodes are the anchors an answer can be checked against.

            

            

              3
              
Deterministic first
A reviewed template answers most questions. The model is the fallback.

            

            

              4
              
Structural embeddings keep you sovereign
FastRP needs no external model and no data leaves the graph.

            

            

              5
              
Show the trace
Which tier answered, which index, which rerank: the explainability the AI Act asks for.

            

          

          
[2:00] Five lessons for EHDS implementers.

        


        
        

          
WHAT GRAPH BUILDERS CAN LEARN FROM THE EHDS

          
Four lessons

          

            

              
Refusing is a feature

              
A wrong answer about a person is worse than no answer.

            

            

              
Policy lives in the graph

              
Next to the data it governs, versioned and queryable.

            

            

              
Identity is a role decision

              
Who may see a person is decided by the session, never by a prompt or a parameter.

            

            

              
Provenance is a node

              
Where a value came from is first-class data, not a log line.
