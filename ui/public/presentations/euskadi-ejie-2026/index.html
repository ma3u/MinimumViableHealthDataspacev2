QUESTION 1 · CHALLENGES

Culture was harder than technology

Four challenges, and where each lesson comes from.

Culture

FROM CATENA-X

Working groups delivered only when top management gave its experts time and a mandate. In a hospital the same holds for clinicians and clinical coders.

Onboarding

FROM CATENA-X

Joining was contracts, identity and a certificate check first, software second. A small participant needs a hosted connector, not its own engineers.

Governance

FROM CATENA-X

Without a rulebook nobody could say who may join, or what happens when a rule is broken. Health adds a layer Catena-X never had: the data permit.

Meaning of the data

FROM OUR EHDS DEMONSTRATOR

Mapping to FHIR, SNOMED CT, ICD-10 and OMOP took more work than the connector. Identity and keys failed more often than the data exchange.

Moving data is easy. Agreeing on what it means is hard.

EJIE Data Space · Euskadi

[1:45] Culture, onboarding and governance are the Catena-X lessons; the meaning of the data is what our EHDS demonstrator taught us. The next slide makes it concrete.

QUESTION 6 · GOVERNANCE · OPEN POINT

Open point: who is the access body?

The Basque Health Data Office was announced in 2025. Its role under the EHDS is not decided yet.

A

A Basque access body

The Basque office is an access body, with a national coordinator (Art. 55(1)).

For: decisions close to the data.

Against: the Ley de Salud Digital must allow it.

B

One national access body

Spain designates one body. The Basque office prepares requests and runs the regional node.

For: one door for applicants.

Against: less regional control.

C

Start now, decide later

The pilot starts under a cooperation agreement. The role is settled when the law passes.

For: the pilot is not blocked.

Against: the role must be revisited.

Spain designates its access bodies by 26 March 2027. The pilot can start under any of the three.

Regulation (EU) 2025/327, Art. 55(1) · Ley de Salud Digital, draft of September 2025

[1:15, four clicks] A regional body with a coordinator, one national body, or start under an agreement and decide later. The pilot does not have to wait.
