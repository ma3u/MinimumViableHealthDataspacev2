Health Dataspace demo hub, Privacy Notice

Last updated 28 October 2026. Controller: Matthias Buchhorn. Contact: matthias.buchhorn@web.de for anything personal, or the public issue tracker at github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2 for everything else.

The demo hub at ehds.mabu.red is an open-source reference implementation of the European Health Data Space (Regulation (EU) 2025/327). It is a demonstration, not a health service. Every patient record on it is synthetic: invented people with invented values. Do not enter real health data.

Signing in with an EUDI Wallet

When you sign in with a European Digital Identity Wallet, the hub asks your wallet for two attributes of your person identification data (PID) and nothing else: your family name and your given name. Your wallet shows you this request, and nothing is shared unless you confirm it there.

The hub compares the name with its synthetic records. If one carries that name, you are signed in to that record; if none does, the sign-in is refused. Either way the name is not stored. Your session carries a pseudonym and the record it opens, not your name. A sign-in in progress is held in the server's memory for at most five minutes and then discarded.

Your wallet does not send the attributes to the hub directly but to a verifier, which checks the wallet's signatures and hands the hub the result. Today that is the EU's EUDI Wallet reference verifier at verifier-backend.eudiw.dev. Once the hub's own registration is complete, it is the hub's own verifier at verifier.ehds.mabu.red, run on the same Azure account as the hub.

Legal basis: your consent, given in the wallet for each sign-in (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it by not signing in again; there is nothing stored to delete.

Demo accounts

The hub also offers fixed demo accounts (operator, clinic, researcher, access body, patient), signed in through Keycloak. They belong to fictional organisations and hold no personal data.

Cookies and local storage

What Why How long
The session cookie, and the sign-in's own short-lived cookies To keep you signed in; strictly necessary At most 8 hours, or until you sign out
The demo persona you chose, in the browser's local storage To show the same demo view on your next visit Until you clear it

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party script on the hub.

Hosting and logs

The hub runs on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe region (the Netherlands). The documentation site runs on GitHub Pages. Both providers may record technical access data, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to operate and secure their service.

The hub's own logs hold technical events and errors. They never contain the attributes from your wallet. They are kept for at most 90 days (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: keeping the demo running and secure).

What the hub never does

  • store your name, or any other attribute from your wallet;
  • ask your wallet for more than your family and given name;
  • hold real health data;
  • share, sell or profile anything about you.

Your rights

You have the rights under Arts. 15 to 21 GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Since the hub keeps nothing that names you, a request is usually answered by saying so; write to the address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state where you live or work.

Changes

This notice changes when the hub changes; the date at the top says when. Its history is in the repository.
