Health Dataspace demo hub, Privacy Notice

Last updated 28 October 2026. Controller: Matthias Buchhorn. Contact: matthias.buchhorn@web.de for anything personal, or the public issue tracker at github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2 for everything else.

The demo hub at ehds.mabu.red is an open-source reference implementation of the European Health Data Space (Regulation (EU) 2025/327). It is a demonstration, not a health service. Every patient record on it is synthetic: invented people with invented values. Do not enter real health data.

Signing in with an EUDI Wallet

When you sign in with a European Digital Identity Wallet, or connect it to an account, the hub asks your wallet for three attributes of your person identification data (PID) and nothing else: your family name, your given name and your birth date. Your wallet shows you this request, and nothing is shared unless you confirm it there.

The hub first compares the name with its two synthetic demo patients. If one carries that name, you are signed in to that record. Otherwise the hub computes a secure hash of your name and birth date and looks for an account that carries the same hash: if one does, you are signed in to it; if none does, the sign-in is refused and you may create an account (see below). The name and the birth date are not stored: they are held in the server's memory only while the hash is computed. Your session carries a pseudonym and the record it opens, not your name. A sign-in in progress is held in the server's memory for at most five minutes and then discarded.

Connecting your wallet to an account

An account the Klarbefund app or a wallet sign-in created (a login kb-… with an empty sandbox record) can be connected to your wallet, so that you sign in to it with your wallet. When you connect it, or create an account from a wallet sign-in, the hub keeps one secure hash per connected account, in the account's entry in the hub's sign-in service (Keycloak), and nothing else of your PID.

Data Stored? Why
Given name, family name, birth date No, in memory while the hash is computed Input of the hash only
The secure hash Yes, one value per connected account, in Keycloak Recognises you at your next wallet sign-in
The key of the hash Yes, in Azure Key Vault, apart from Keycloak Without it the hash can be neither computed nor checked

The hash is a keyed one-way function (HMAC-SHA256): it cannot be turned back into your name or birth date, and without the key it cannot even be checked against a guess. It is still personal data, pseudonymised in the sense of Art. 4(5) GDPR, because it lets the hub recognise you. Two people with the same name and birth date would have the same hash; the second one to connect is refused and told why.

Legal basis: your consent, given when you connect the wallet or create the account (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You withdraw it with Disconnect wallet on the page where you connected it, which removes the hash at once. Deleting the account removes it too.

Your wallet does not send the attributes to the hub directly but to a verifier, which checks the wallet's signatures and hands the hub the result. Today that is the EU's EUDI Wallet reference verifier at verifier-backend.eudiw.dev. Once the hub's own registration is complete, it is the hub's own verifier at verifier.ehds.mabu.red, run on the same Azure account as the hub.

Legal basis for a sign-in: your consent, given in the wallet for each sign-in (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it by not signing in again; a sign-in stores nothing of your PID unless you connect your wallet to an account, below.

Demo accounts

The hub also offers fixed demo accounts (operator, clinic, researcher, access body, patient), signed in through Keycloak. They belong to fictional organisations and hold no personal data.

Cookies and local storage

What Why How long
The session cookie, and the sign-in's own short-lived cookies To keep you signed in; strictly necessary At most 8 hours, or until you sign out
The demo persona you chose, in the browser's local storage To show the same demo view on your next visit Until you clear it

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party script on the hub.

Hosting and logs

The hub runs on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe region (the Netherlands). The documentation site runs on GitHub Pages. Both providers may record technical access data, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to operate and secure their service.

The hub's own logs hold technical events and errors. They never contain the attributes from your wallet, nor their hash. They are kept for at most 90 days (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: keeping the demo running and secure).

What the hub never does

  • store your name, your birth date, or any other attribute from your wallet; only their secure hash, and only on an account you connected;
  • ask your wallet for more than your family name, given name and birth date;
  • hold real health data;
  • share, sell or profile anything about you.

Your rights

You have the rights under Arts. 15 to 21 GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. The hub keeps nothing that names you in clear; if you connected a wallet, it keeps the secure hash on that account, which you remove yourself with Disconnect wallet or by deleting the account. For anything else, write to the address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state where you live or work.
