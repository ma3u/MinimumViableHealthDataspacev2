# Internal notes to the Simpl-Live and EHDS partners report

Moved out of [the planning report](../../planning/future/simpl-live-and-ehds-partners-2026-11.md)
on 2026-11-05. These are Sopra Steria positioning notes; no page links here. Contacts were
researched on 2026-11-05 from public professional sources only (official pages, notices,
event speaker pages); each row names its source and the year it shows. Only functional
mailboxes are given. Roles marked "check" may have changed since the source.

## Positioning

1. **The direct route into the EHDS2 integration is Sopra Steria's own seat.** Sopra Steria
   Benelux leads [InfrateX](https://www.soprasteria.com/newsroom/press-releases/details/infratex-consortium-led-by-sopra-steria-awarded-simpl-a-major-multiple-framework-contract-by-european-commission-s-dg-connect)
   on the Simpl framework contract CNECT/2022/OP/0132
   ([TED award 623496-2023](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/623496-2023)). Which
   contractor delivers [EHDS2 in Simpl-Live wave 1](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/programme/simpl-live/wave-1)
   is not public: ask the InfrateX / Simpl account lead at Sopra Steria Benelux first whether
   InfrateX has or bids a specific contract for the EHDS2 integration, and offer the hub as
   the health test bed. Clear the outward steps of the plan internally at the same time.
   (Public report plan step 1.)
2. **[EHDS Stakeholder Forum](https://www.ehtel.eu/media-room/latest-news/369-get-involved-in-the-future-of-the-ehds-express-your-interest.html)**
   (deadline 10 Dec 2026): apply as Sopra Steria under industry, or via a partner
   organisation. (Public plan step 7.)
3. **[AMI BOAS](https://health-data-hub.fr/ami-boas)** (4 Jan 2027, 12:00 Paris, French lead
   required): ask Sopra Steria France, after point 1, whether to lead. (Public plan step 17.)
4. **[Discussion #895](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/discussions/895)**
   names no employer; add a Sopra Steria line only after point 1 clears it.
5. **Partner analysis, 41 consortia:** Sopra Steria appears in none. Atos/Eviden, Capgemini
   and Fujitsu are absent from the secondary-use ones too; large integrators win the
   Commission's tenders and framework contracts instead (InfrateX,
   [EC-SANTE/2026/OP/0035](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/533039-2026)). So the
   realistic grant role is a technology partner next to an SME that wins repeatedly
   (empirica, The Hyve, UpHill, Gnomon), and the tender role stays with Sopra Steria.
6. **Not confirmed:** whether Sopra Steria has done Simpl-Live work under the framework.
7. **[sphin-X e.V.](https://www.sphin-x.de):** Sopra Steria is not a member
   ([MII deck, 25 Mar 2026](https://www.medizininformatik-initiative.de/sites/default/files/2026-03/S10.5%20_Bader.pdf));
   joining is a low-cost entry to German health industry
   ([membership](https://www.sphin-x.de/partner-und-mitmachen/)).
8. **Correction to the report (fixed 2026-11-05):** HADEA/2024/OP/0034 is "Support to
   stakeholders on secondary use" (IDOM with IACS: Stakeholder Forum, Stakeholders'
   Community, Health Policy Platform), not the HDAB community of practice. The HDAB
   community of practice secretariat is Intellera's contract (with empirica, PredictBy and
   Cecoforma, per the October tenders note; not re-verified).
