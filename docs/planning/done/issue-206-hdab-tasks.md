# Issue #206: HDAB tasks under Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (done 2026-09-25)

Plan: the access body can receive, assess, decide, supervise, publish and
report, as Art. 57, 61 to 64, 67 to 69, 72 and 73 require, with the clocks
those articles set. Seven milestones, shipped in PRs #207 to #212 (17 and 18
September 2026) and the closing PR of 25 September 2026.

| Milestone                           | What exists                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M0 citations                        | Adopted article numbers everywhere; a unit test fails on a proposal number                                                                                                                                                                          |
| M1 applications                     | The eleven items of Art. 67(2), the researcher's form on `/applications`, completeness on every record, the public list on `/permits`                                                                                                               |
| M2 decision and register            | The Art. 68 decision with criteria, conditions, validity; the 68(3) statistical alternative; the 68(4) clock with the incompleteness notice (four weeks) and the one extension; the public register                                                 |
| M3 the permit gates the data        | Transfers, queries and analytics refuse without a valid permit; events carry the permit; retention of one year with a purge that deletes nothing younger                                                                                            |
| M4 supervision                      | Findings with the four-week views window and the GDPR flag; measures (warning, revocation, exclusion, fine) published; requests for information answered on record                                                                                  |
| M5 health data requests             | Anonymised statistics only; the trusted holder answers for its own datasets (Art. 72)                                                                                                                                                               |
| M6 transparency and reporting       | The Art. 58(1) page `/information`; results within 18 months on `/permits`, `/information` and in the report; the Art. 59 report with results, fees and fines                                                                                       |
| M7 fees, templates, trusted holders | Fee estimate and fee on every issued permit and approved request, reduced categories; AlphaKlinik Berlin seeded as trusted holder. The Art. 70 templates arrive by implementing act (26 March 2027); until then the forms carry the article's items |

Open by nature of the regulation: the Art. 70 templates, cross-border
forwarding of one application to several bodies (Art. 67(3), HealthData@EU).
