# EHDS article numbering: proposal vs adopted

Most EHDS article references in this repository are the numbers from the 2022
proposal, [COM(2022) 197 final](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:52022PC0197). The Regulation was renumbered before adoption, and
the adopted numbers in [Regulation (EU) 2025/327](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng) are different, in places by more
than twenty.

This matters in one specific room. A regulator who knows the Regulation reads
"secure processing environment, Art. 50" and sees [Art. 50](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_50) of the adopted text,
which is _Applicability to health data holders_. The claim looks wrong even
though the substance is right.

**New material uses the adopted numbers.** Existing material has not been
rewritten: roughly 48 files carry proposal numbers, and a mass renumber before a
demo is the kind of change that quietly breaks something. Use this table when
reading them, and correct a file whenever you are editing it anyway.

No successor: proposal Art. 40 (data altruism in health) and Art. 54 (mutual
recognition) were absorbed elsewhere. Proposal Art. 48 (public sector bodies without a data permit) was dropped: a public sector body processes health data only under a data permit or a health data request, like any health data user ([Art. 54](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_54) as adopted), and [Art. 53](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_53)(2) reserves purposes (a) to (c) to it. New in the adopted text with no proposal
counterpart: [Art. 56](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_56) (Union health data access service), [Art. 61](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_61) (duties of
health data users), [Art. 70](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_70) (templates), [Art. 71](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_71) (opt-out from secondary use),
[Art. 81](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_81) (right to complain).

## The retention claim, resolved

[`docs/audit-retention-policy.md`](audit-retention-policy.md) used to cite "EHDS Art. 50(3)" for a ten-year
retention duty on access events. Nothing sourced the ten years. The adopted
[Art. 73](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#art_73)(1)(e) requires access logs for at least one year, and the policy now
keeps twelve months as that floor ([#694](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/issues/694)).
