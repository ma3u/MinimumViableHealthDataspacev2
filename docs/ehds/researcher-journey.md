# The researcher's journey: from registration to an audited result

A researcher at PharmaCo Research AG wants to use health data held by
AlphaKlinik Berlin. The Regulation ([EU 2025/327](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng))
calls this secondary use, [Chapter IV](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#cpt_IV): the holder describes its data, the
user applies to the health data access body (MedReg DE), the access body
decides, and the data is processed only in a secure processing environment,
from which only aggregates leave. This page walks the journey step by step, as
the second diagram on the [start page](https://ma3u.github.io/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/) draws it, and
says for each step what the hub implements, what is planned and what is not
planned. Where the Regulation leaves the how open, the hub follows the TEHDAS2
guidelines ([the mapping](tehdas2-mapping.md)).

All organisations are fictional and all data is synthetic.

**Status**, checked on 14 November 2026 against
[the article table](regulation-implementation.md) (checked against the code on
27 October, [#694](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/issues/694)),
the TEHDAS2 ratings ([ADR-086](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/blob/main/docs/ADRs/ADR-086-tehdas2-ratings-after-the-requirement-check.md))
and the open issues:

- **Implemented**: it works end to end and a test shows it.
- **Partial**: part of it works; the next column says what is missing.
- **Planned**: not there yet; the issue says when, by its priority.
- **Not planned**: decided against or out of scope, with the reason.

## Signing in

The column "Sign in as" names the demo user for the step:

- **Static demo** (GitHub Pages): no password; pick the user in the persona
  menu.
- **Local stack** (`http://localhost:3003`): the password is the username.
- **Live hub** (`https://ehds.mabu.red`): the demo users of the realm keep
  their published password, the username. `edcadmin` is the exception: its
  password is generated and kept in Key Vault, because the administrator
  reads every record (see the
  [realm runbook](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/blob/main/docs/knowledge/runbooks/keycloak-realm-drift.md)).

## Not planned

From [ADR-083](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/blob/main/docs/ADRs/ADR-083-what-the-hub-takes-over-from-tehdas2.md), decision 7:

- **Collecting fees and fines.** The hub computes fees and records fines with
  fictional amounts; it collects no money.
- **Access from third countries.** Transfers outside the EU and EEA stay
  refused by the policy constraints of [ADR-077](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/blob/main/docs/ADRs/ADR-077-a-minimal-set-of-standard-data-sharing-policies.md); there is no application
  path for applicants outside it. Third countries join HealthData@EU from
  26 March 2035 at the earliest.
- **The framework for collaboration.** Ethics review and the protection of
  intellectual property are not modelled.

Deferred, so planned but later (P3): anonymisation beyond suppressing groups
under five, by generalisation and synthetic data
([#999](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/issues/999)), and returning enriched datasets to
the access body, after its final guideline
([#998](https://github.com/ma3u/MinimumViableHealthDataspacev2/issues/998)).
